# Code of conduct

OpenRV follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable anywhere in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anything else run in the project's name.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately at [report@openrv.app](mailto:report@openrv.app). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers will review each report and act as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a private word up to a ban from the project.
